RIDGE RUNNER

Bush pilot courier. You fly a battered little cargo plane out of Pine Hollow strip, threading glowing survey rings through the valley, and putting her down on remote dirt strips to drop the mail. Fuel is short, the airframe is old, and the mountains do not forgive.

Fly all three legs — rings, then a landing at each strip — to finish the route. Land gently on a runway to refuel and patch the airframe. Fly through every ring for the full score.
